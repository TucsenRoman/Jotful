1 · Hold

Same button, same spot as peek. Halo pulses with voice level. Haptic: light on press.

2a · Drag out

Mic stays put. A faint ring appears 88pt out; a filled circle grows with the finger's distance. Drag back to shrink.

2b · Meets the ring

Circle reaches the ring: they snap and lock. Haptic: rigid. Then everything shrinks back into the button.

3 · Locked

Finger off: the button is now a stop square. Tap saves. Haptic: success on save.

4 · Cancel

Swipe the sheet down by the grabber to discard. Toast on home offers Undo.
