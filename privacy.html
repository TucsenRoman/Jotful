Privacy policy · September 22, 2026

Your thoughts stay yours.

Information stored by the app

Unsorted stores the notes and thoughts you create locally on your device. The current version has no accounts and no cloud sync.

Information we collect

Unsorted does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use your notes or personal information for analytics, advertising, tracking, or profiling.

Optional voice input

When you tap Voice, Unsorted asks for microphone permission and requests on-device speech recognition only when your iPhone supports it. The app does not upload or retain your audio or transcription. If on-device recognition is unavailable, you can continue typing.

Optional external actions

When you explicitly tap Search or Ask AI, Unsorted opens your browser with only the thought you selected. That action is governed by the privacy policy of the browser, search provider, or AI service you use. Unsorted does not receive the result or retain a copy of the information sent through that handoff.

Data deletion

You may delete an individual thought within the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored information from the device, subject to device and operating-system backup settings.

Children

Unsorted is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
