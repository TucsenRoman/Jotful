# Unsorted Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Unsorted is an offline-first notes application. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information stored by the app

The notes and thoughts you create in Unsorted are stored locally on your device using the app's local database. Unsorted does not provide user accounts or cloud synchronization in the current version.

## Information we collect

Unsorted does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use your notes or personal information for analytics, advertising, tracking, or profiling.

## Optional voice input

When you tap Voice, Unsorted asks for microphone permission and requests on-device speech recognition only when your iPhone supports it. The app does not upload or retain your audio or transcription. If on-device recognition is unavailable, voice input is unavailable and you can continue typing.

## Optional external actions

When you explicitly tap **Search web** or **Ask AI**, Unsorted opens your browser with only the thought you selected. That action is governed by the privacy policy of the browser, search provider, or AI service you use. Unsorted does not receive the result or retain a copy of the information sent through that external action.

## Data deletion

You may delete an individual thought from within the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored information from the device, subject to your device and operating-system backup settings.

## Children

Unsorted is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. A material change to how information is handled will be reflected in an updated app release and this policy.
