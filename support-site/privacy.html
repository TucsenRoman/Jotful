Privacy policy · October 10, 2026

Your thoughts stay yours.

Information stored by the app

The thoughts, recordings, photos, videos, and drafts you create in Jotful are stored locally on your device. The current version has no accounts and no cloud sync.

Information we collect

Jotful does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use your notes, media, or personal information for analytics, advertising, tracking, or profiling.

Voice input and audio recordings

When you tap Voice, Jotful asks for microphone permission and uses on-device speech recognition only. Dictation and transcripts are never uploaded. If on-device recognition is unavailable, you can keep typing. Audio recordings you choose to save stay on your device.

Camera and photo library

When you choose to take a photo or video, Jotful asks for camera access, and microphone access for video sound. When you choose to add from your library, Jotful asks for photo library access so it can show your photos and videos in its picker. Only the items you pick are added, and they stay on your device.

Optional external actions

When you explicitly tap Search web or Ask AI, Jotful opens your browser with only the thought you selected. That action is governed by the privacy policy of the browser, search provider, or AI service you use. Jotful does not receive the result or keep a copy of what is sent.

Data deletion

You may delete an individual thought within the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored information from the device, subject to device and operating-system backup settings.

Children

Jotful is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
